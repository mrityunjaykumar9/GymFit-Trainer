# Privacy Policy for Free Gym Trainer

*Last updated: [DATE]*

This privacy policy explains how Free Gym Trainer ("the App") handles information.

## Data stored on your device

Free Gym Trainer stores all fitness data locally on your device, including your profile
(name, age, gender, height, weight, goals), workout history, nutrition logs, water intake,
body measurements, and progress photos (if used). This data is **not** transmitted to us or
to any server we operate, because the App does not use a backend server. Uninstalling the
App or clearing its data permanently deletes this information.

## Advertising data

The App is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device
identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location, and app usage data to serve
and measure ads, in accordance with Google's own privacy policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device's Google settings
(Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization).

## Permissions used

- **Notifications** — to send optional workout, water, and nutrition reminders you configure.
- **Internet / network state** — required only to load ads; not used to transmit your fitness data anywhere.

## Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes
constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
