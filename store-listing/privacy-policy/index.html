Privacy Policy

How GymFit Trainer handles your information

Last updated October 9, 2026

About this app

This privacy policy applies to the Android application GymFit Trainer: Workout Log (Google Play package name com.freegymtrainer.app), published on Google Play under the developer name mrityunjay_kumar23 by Mrityunjay Kumar. It covers the app as distributed on Google Play and describes how it handles information for every version of the app.

The short version: GymFit Trainer has no backend server and no login. Your profile, workouts, and nutrition logs stay on your device — we never see them.

Data stored on your device

GymFit Trainer stores all fitness data locally on your device, including your profile (name, age, gender, height, weight, goals), workout history, nutrition logs, water intake, and body measurements. This data is not transmitted to us or to any server we operate, because the app does not use a backend server. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data permanently deletes this information.

Advertising data

The app does not charge for use and is supported by ads served through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location, and app usage data to serve and measure ads, in accordance with Google's own privacy policy.

You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device's Google settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization).

Permissions used

  • Notifications — to send optional workout, water, and nutrition reminders you configure yourself.
  • Internet / network state — required only to load ads; never used to transmit your fitness data anywhere.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
